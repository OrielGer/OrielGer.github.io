Approval cards

Nothing risky happens without asking you.

Before it sends an email or a message, places a call, runs a shell command or changes files outside its folder, a Bot stops and shows a card: one sentence that says what is about to happen, with the details under it.

  • Allow once Just this action.
  • Always in this chat For actions that repeat. Email and phone calls have no "always": they ask every time.
  • Not this time Nothing happens, and the Bot carries on without it.
  • From anywhere Answer ordinary cards straight from the notification, on the desktop or the phone.

Engines

Your subscriptions. Your sign-ins.

Bots work through each engine's official tool, with your own sign-in on your PC. No API keys required (you can add them if you want). Kaveret never sees your credentials and holds no one's AI account.

  • A model per Bot or per chat and switch mid-conversation: the context carries over.
  • Group chats several Bots, even on different engines, in one conversation.
  • Hit a usage limit? Turn on auto-continue and the request carries on with another vendor's engine you have signed in. Off by default, and it never switches to another account of the same vendor.
  • Everything in one place Settings → Engines shows each engine: connected, up to date, and on which plan.

On your phone

Your Bots in your pocket, no server needed.

Your PC stays the home of your Bots, and your phone reaches it privately over Tailscale: no open ports, no router setup.

  1. Install Tailscale on the PC and the phone, signed in to the same account on both.
  2. Turn it on in Kaveret Settings → Devices → Access from your phone and other computers.
  3. Scan the QR code Kaveret opens on the phone and signs in. Install it as an app (PWA) or use the experimental Android app, with notifications.

When the PC is off or asleep, the phone can't reach the Bots. Want them always available? Run your own server.

Safety and privacy

Yours. On your PC. With your OK.

There is no Kaveret cloud and no telemetry. Your chats, files and vault stay on your PC, and your prompts go only to the engine you picked.

Runs on your PC

Everything is stored with you (or on your own server, if you choose). The app checks GitHub Releases for updates, and that's it.

Approval cards

A consequential action stops at a card. Per Bot, you choose how much it may do alone: always ask, auto-review or full access.

Outside content is data, not orders

Messages, emails, web pages and webhooks can't approve anything. A turn that read them gets no automatic approvals, and strangers on external channels never reach a Bot.

An encrypted vault

Passwords and tokens live in an encrypted vault (AES-256-GCM). When a Bot signs in to a site, the app types the value into the page; the model never sees it.

Redaction

Secrets are masked in chats, streams and logs.

Payments always ask

Paying or checking out always needs your explicit approval, and you can set spending limits.

Full access, with limits

You can let a trusted Bot skip routine questions. What always waits for your approval:

  • Payments and purchases
  • Typing a secret into a new site
  • Revealing a secret
  • A message, call or email to anyone but you
  • Deleting or changing files outside the Bot's folder
  • Installing software
  • Changing system settings
  • Anything irreversible or that costs money

FAQ

Questions and answers

Is it free?

The app is open source under the MIT License. You use your own AI subscriptions (or free local models); phone calls (Twilio) and your own server cost what those services charge.

Does my data go to a Kaveret server?

No. There is no Kaveret cloud and no telemetry. Your chats, files and vault stay on your PC (or on your own server). Your prompts go to the AI engine you picked, as with that engine's own app; the desktop app also checks GitHub Releases for updates.

Does Kaveret hold my AI account?

No. Each engine runs through its own official tool, signed in by you on your PC. Kaveret never sees your credentials and never switches accounts by itself.

Which engines?

Claude, ChatGPT (Codex), Grok, Google Gemini, local models (Ollama), and other ACP agents. Google Gemini works in two ways: through Google Antigravity with your Google subscription (subject to Google's terms, and the app shows a warning), or through the Gemini CLI with an API key from Google AI Studio (free with limits, or paid).

Which devices?

Windows 10/11 has an installer (tester build). A Mac version is coming soon. On your phone: the experimental Android app or the PWA, both connecting to Kaveret on your PC. The interface is in Hebrew, English and more languages.

Why does Windows warn me when I install?

The installer is not yet code-signed with a commercial certificate, so SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Does it work offline?

With local models (Ollama) the Bots run without the internet; tools that need the web (browsing, email, phone) don't.
