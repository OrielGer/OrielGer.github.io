Phone setup guide

Use Kaveret from your phone

Your bots live on your computer. Your phone talks to them through Tailscale, a free app that links your own devices privately. There is nothing to change on your router. It takes about ten minutes, once.

What you need

  • Kaveret installed and open on your computer. The computer has to be on and awake whenever you use your phone.
  • A free Tailscale account. You will sign in to the same account on the computer and on the phone.

Steps

  1. On the computer, in Kaveret, open Settings › Devices › Access from your phone and other computers. If Tailscale isn't installed yet, Kaveret shows how to install it (on Windows, with one click). When Tailscale opens, sign in.
  2. On the phone, install Tailscale from the App Store or Google Play and sign in to the same Tailscale account.
  3. Back on the computer, turn the access on. If Tailscale asks for a one-time approval, click the button Kaveret shows and approve on the page that opens. Kaveret then shows a QR code.
  4. On the phone, scan the QR code with the camera. Kaveret opens and signs you in.

Keep it on your home screen

  1. iPhone: after scanning, in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android (test app): on the phone, download Kaveret-version-android.apk from the releases page and open it. When asked, allow installs from this source, then tap Install. On first start, enter the address shown in Settings › Devices.

If the phone can't reach Kaveret

Check that the computer is on and awake, that Kaveret is open, and that Tailscale is connected on both devices with the same account. Then scan the QR code again.

Rather not install anything on the phone? Kaveret can also talk to you on Telegram, with a one-time setup inside the app.
