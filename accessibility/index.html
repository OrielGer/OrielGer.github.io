Accessibility statement

Last updated: 14 November 2026

We want everyone to be able to use Kaveret and this website, including people who use a screen reader, only a keyboard, large text or high contrast.

1. This website

We built kaveret.ai to meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1), the level the Israeli standard SI 5568 asks for. What it has today:

  • A "Skip to content" link at the top of every page, and every link and button works with the keyboard alone, with a visible focus ring.
  • Clear headings and page regions, so a screen reader can move from section to section.
  • Every screenshot is described in words. Pictures that are only decoration are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text can be enlarged in the browser to 200% without losing content. Light and dark follow your system.
  • If your system asks for less motion, the page doesn't animate and the demo shows a still picture.
  • Everything can be read without JavaScript. The site loads nothing from other websites and sets no cookies.
  • Every page is in English and in Hebrew (right to left).

2. The Kaveret app

  • Settings › General has text size, high contrast and reduce motion.
  • Buttons in the app have names that a screen reader can read, and an automatic check looks for missing ones in every version.
  • The app works with the keyboard, and you can talk to it with the microphone instead of typing.
  • Its screens are available in eight languages, in both directions.

3. What isn't there yet

  • No outside expert has audited the website or the app yet, and we haven't yet tested the whole chat with a screen reader. We plan to.
  • The text inside the screenshots can't be read by a screen reader. The description next to each picture says what it shows.
  • Pages we link to, such as the AI companies, Google, Tailscale and GitHub, are run by others and have their own accessibility.

4. Tell us

If something on the website or in the app is hard to use, or you need information in another way, write to support@kaveret.ai. Tell us which page or screen, and what happened. We read every message.
